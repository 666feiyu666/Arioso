Do not begin with a full ensemble attack or a separate introductory theme.

Reassemble the orchestra in audible stages: pizzicato low strings and hand drums, then woodwind ornaments, then sweeping violins and horn responses. Include one short withdrawal to solo oboe over low strings, then launch the final acceleration of energy without changing the underlying 96 BPM pulse.
