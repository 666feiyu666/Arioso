# DH6012-project

## MoodMic: Project Brief

**MoodMic creates a single piece of music or a personal album inspired by how you feel.**

### Purpose

Create a dedicated interface for the DH6012 course project, building on the existing application's music creation capabilities. MoodMic should give those capabilities a clear identity and an inviting experience that makes people want to explore, create, and listen.

The current interface feels rough and resembles an industrial production workflow. The project goal is to make music creation feel personal, expressive, and approachable, with the user's mood providing the starting point.

### Intended Audience

MoodMic is for people who want to turn a feeling into music, including users with no musical training. For the course presentation, classmates and assessors should be able to understand its purpose quickly and follow the experience without a technical explanation.

### Core Experience

Users should be able to:

- Express their current mood and the feeling they want their music to convey.
- Choose between creating one piece and creating an album.
- Clarify their creative wishes and understand the proposed direction before creation begins.
- Follow the creation process with clear feedback about what is happening and what to do next.
- Listen to the resulting music and use the existing options for reviewing, managing, and exporting their work.

A single piece should feel like a complete expression of a moment. An album should feel like a connected collection with a shared emotional direction, while allowing individual tracks to have their own character.

### Design Goals

**Invite interaction.** The first encounter should create curiosity and make the next action easy to understand. Users should feel encouraged to try MoodMic rather than needing instructions before they can begin.

**Make the experience personal.** Language, visual identity, and interaction should support the connection between feeling and music. Users should recognise their own creative intention in the journey and its outcome.

**Establish a polished identity.** MoodMic should have a coherent and memorable presentation, with consistent typography, colour, spacing, and visual hierarchy suitable for a course demonstration.

**Keep choices approachable.** Users should understand the difference between a piece and an album, and be able to express preferences without specialist vocabulary. Existing depth should remain accessible without overwhelming the initial experience.

**Support confidence throughout.** Progress, waiting, completion, and errors should be understandable. Users should know whether an action is needed and how to continue.

**Give listening a central role.** Completed music should feel like the result of a creative experience. The presentation should encourage users to listen and explore their work.

### Scope and Boundaries

The deliverable is a course-specific MoodMic interface that preserves the application's existing functionality. Single-piece and album creation are the two primary user-facing choices. Other existing capabilities should remain available; this brief does not authorise their removal.

The focus is product presentation, interaction quality, and the overall creative experience. This document records goals only. Technical architecture, implementation methods, detailed screen designs, and additional music-generation capabilities are outside its scope.

### Completion Criteria

The interface is ready for the course project when:

- A first-time viewer can explain what MoodMic does and identify how to begin.
- Users can choose either a piece or an album and complete the journey from expressing a mood to listening to the result.
- The presentation feels cohesive, inviting, and appropriate for a creative music application.
- Existing functionality is preserved and remains usable.
- Feedback makes the application's state and the user's next step clear.
- The course demonstration communicates the relationship between the user's mood, their creative choices, and the resulting music.
